1. शरीर-शुद्धिः (Śarīra-śuddhiḥ)

अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा । यः स्मरेत् पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः शुचिः ॥
apavitraḥ pavitro vā sarvāvasthāṃ gato'pi vā | yaḥ smaret puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ sa bāhyābhyantaraḥ śuciḥ ||
🤲
Physical Action:

Take a spoon of clean water with uddharaṇe, sprinkle on your head while reciting.

💡
Brahmachāri Secret Power:

Before entering the sacred ritual, this mantra reminds you that remembering the Lord clears away all tiredness and mental fog instantly!

🖐️ Finger Grip for Prāṇāyāma (Prāṇāyāma Mudrā):

• Right Thumb gently closes the right nostril.
• Ring & Little fingers gently close the left nostril.
• Inhale (Pūraka) through the left nostril while mentally chanting the sacred Vyāhṛtis.
• Hold (Kumbhaka) closing both nostrils while reciting Gāyatrī with Śiras.
• Exhale (Recaka) slowly releasing the right nostril.
